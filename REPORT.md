## Background

The scaffold (PROPOSAL.md, former SPEC.md) asked three things: train
a small CLIP-style dual encoder end to end; wire one open
vision-language model (VLM) into a question-answering service; and
compare that VLM with a text-only pipeline on the same document
questions, with one failure class written up. CLIP (Radford et al.
2021) trains an image encoder and a text encoder so matching pairs
land close together under a contrastive (InfoNCE) loss; LLaVA (Liu et
al. 2023) joins a CLIP image encoder to a language model through a
projector. level-1-learn/ANATOMY.md walks that design part by part as
a document inspection.

No VLM was ever downloaded or run, at any level (docs/decisions/0001).
The vision route in every result is a stand-in: the Learn encoder
retrieves the nearest of 12 captions and a keyword composer answers,
labelled `learn_encoder_stand_in` in the code and results. Every
dataset is synthetic. Nothing in this report transfers to a real VLM,
to natural images or to real documents.

## Method

- Learn (level-1-learn/): a NumPy CLIP-style dual encoder with
  hand-written backpropagation (gradients checked against finite
  differences), embed dim 32, temperature 0.1, Adam, 2,500 steps of 12
  pairs, on 1,200 synthetic train pairs and 120 held-out pairs over 12
  colour and shape combinations (16 by 16 images, vocabulary 9),
  master seed 42. Metric: held-out Recall at 1, both directions.
- Build (level-2-build/): a deterministic QA service on that encoder,
  48 synthetic template questions (12 combinations by 4 types), every
  output checked by a P14-style schema validator, scored by P06-style
  exact match and token F1.
- Deep (level-3-deep/): 24 synthetic documents (six each with 1 to 4
  figure items), each a 16 by 16 figure plus a text layer, 6 question
  types, 144 cases. Vision route: the Build service on the figure.
  Text route: a hand-written parser over the text layer. Same scoring.
- Analysis (analysis/): five encoder seeds (42, 1, 2, 3, 4); percentile
  bootstrap 95% intervals (10,000 resamples; per type over the 24
  cases, overall over the 24 documents as clusters); exact McNemar
  tests on the paired per-case rows; a non-learned enumeration baseline
  that counts 4-connected non-background regions in each figure.
- Compute and versions: CPU only. The recorded runs used the Muse VM,
  Python 3.12.3, NumPy 1.26.4. CI reruns everything on Python 3.11
  (NumPy 1.26.4) and 3.12 (NumPy 2.5.4) and requires every results file
  to come out byte-identical. The full regeneration takes under a
  minute.

## Threats to validity

- Construct. The "vision route" is the Learn encoder plus a keyword
  composer, not a VLM. No result transfers to a real VLM.
- Internal. The text layer carries the answers to 4 of 6 question
  types by design. Count is constant in code. Three-item figures have
  touching shapes.
- Statistical. 24 cases per type. Cases cluster within documents, so
  the overall McNemar test (27 against 72 discordant pairs, p 6.9e-6)
  is optimistic; the cluster-bootstrap interval is the honest overall
  figure.
- External. All data is synthetic 16 by 16 images with fixed
  vocabularies.
- Image-to-text Recall at 5 equals Recall at 1 by construction (the
  held-out captions repeat), so it is not reported as a separate
  result.

## Conclusion

A hand-built NumPy CLIP learns colour and shape on a synthetic
teaching set. Wrapped in a QA service and set against a text parser
on synthetic documents, it pays only where the answer lives in the
pixels (colour), loses everywhere the text layer carries the answer,
and cannot count, because its composer always answers "one" while the
pixels carry most counts. The question the project was set, a real
VLM against a text pipeline, is still open.

### What was fixed in this change

1. **Single seed, so no variance reported.** `analysis/run_seeds.py` reruns Learn and the Deep vision route with encoder seeds 42, 1, 2, 3 and 4 (`analysis/results_seeds.json`). Seed 42 reproduces the recorded runs exactly.
   - Learn image-to-text Recall at 1 is 0.990 mean (sd 0.011, range 0.975 to 1.000).
   - Text-to-image Recall at 1 is 1.000 at every seed.
   - Deep vision exact match is 0.371 mean (sd 0.017, range 0.354 to 0.396). The recorded 0.354 sits at the low end of that range.
   - Colour (0.750), count (0.250) and title (0.000) do not move across seeds. Shape, both and left_shape move between 0.375 and 0.458.
   - The text route is a deterministic parser, so it does not depend on the seed.
2. **No uncertainty or paired test.** `analysis/paired_stats.py` (`analysis/results_stats.json`) works on the 144 recorded per-case rows.
   - Per type, it gives percentile bootstrap 95% CIs by resampling the 24 cases of that type (10,000 resamples). Example: vision colour exact match 0.750, CI 0.583 to 0.917.
   - Overall, it resamples the 24 documents (cluster bootstrap): vision 0.354, CI 0.229 to 0.486. The vision-minus-text difference has a CI of -0.438 to -0.181.
   - The exact McNemar test finds every per-type difference significant. The largest p is 0.0039, for both, where vision alone was right on 9 cases and text alone on 0.
   - The overall McNemar test (27 against 72 discordant pairs, p 6.9e-6) treats cases as independent, but the six cases of a document are not. Read it as optimistic. The cluster-bootstrap interval is the honest overall figure.
   - The dashboard recomputes all of this in the browser, and `site/src/lab.check.mjs` asserts that it matches.
3. **Image-to-text R@1 equals R@5 (0.983), not analysed.** The seed-42 confusion matrix (`results_seeds.json`, `learn_confusion_seed_42`) shows the two misses: one red circle retrieved as red square, and one green square retrieved as green circle. Both are circle and square confusions within one colour. R@5 equals R@1 by construction: the held-out captions repeat 10 times per combination, so an image's top five captions are five copies of one caption. Image-to-text R@5 carries no information in this design and should not be reported as a separate result.
4. **Counting failure is true by construction, with no baseline.** `analysis/count_baseline.py` counts 4-connected non-background regions, with no learning, and scores 0.750 exact match on the 24 count questions (`analysis/results_count_baseline.json`).
   - It gets 1.000 on one-, two- and four-item figures and 0.000 on three-item figures.
   - **New threat found:** the three-item slots (x = 2, 7, 12, half-size 2) draw neighbouring shapes touching, so their pixels merge. This flaw is in the v1.0 document set itself.
   - Reading: the pixels carry most counts, so the constant-one composer is the main cause of the failure class. This is a ceiling check on fixed slots, not a counting model.
5. **Literature grounding.** Primary references were added to DOSSIER.md: CLIP, InfoNCE (CPC), LLaVA and LLaVA-1.5, Adam, McNemar and the bootstrap.
6. **Test ergonomics.** The suite runs as scripts, as CI does. The README said this (Appendix B). The new `tests/test_analysis.py` uses `test_*` functions, so pytest collects it too.
7. **Dashboard self-check.** `site/src/lab.check.mjs` runs inside `npm run build` and checks the following:
   - The JavaScript ports reproduce the deterministic text route exactly: 0.666667 exact match and 0.75 token F1, row by row over 144 cases.
   - They also reproduce the gold labels and the token counts (17 visual tokens, 22 to 25 unified tokens).
   - The trainer passes the repo's loss oracles and a finite-difference gradient check.
   - The README benchmark table matches the results files.
